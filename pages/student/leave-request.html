PDF or image files allowed. Max 4MB.

Leave Policy Guidelines

  • Advance Filing: Outing applications must be raised at least 24 hours prior to departure.
  • Approvals Queue: Warden is the primary approver. For leave > 3 days, HOD approval is also required.
  • Outing Gatepass: Once approved, your campus digital ID is updated, enabling exit gate clearance.
Warning: Leaving without approved gatepass counts as Illegal Absence, which generates automated alerts to parents and HODs.
