File Maintenance Complaint

Report hostel room and amenity issues to maintenance personnel

Supported formats: JPG, PNG. Max size 2MB.

Important Tips

  • High Priority: Use this only for absolute disruptions (complete power outage, plumbing overflow, broken locks).
  • Attach Images: Attaching photos helps repair technicians diagnose the issues before arriving at your room.
  • Verification: Once resolved, you will receive an alert. You can reopen or verify complaints in the Timeline tab.
